James has been teaching Improv for over 7 years in Leamington Spa, and loving every minute of it. He's mostly been short-form based, coaching quick fire Improv teams taking shows to Brighton and Edinburgh.

Known for workshops covering both the basics, and some more experimental ideas, aiming for enjoying, trying new things, and learning, in that order.
