Kate trained as a performer at Drama Studios London. She established a career in sketch comedy before joining _City Improv_ for 6 years, and helped to run their Improv festival in Shoreditch, before moving to the Cotswolds. She has taken shows to Edinburgh, Brighton and Camden fringe festivals.

John has been improvising for almost 10 years, in and around Cheltenham.

Kate and John both regularly teach and perform shortform with _Comic Sans Sript_, and longform with _Shatterprov_.
