Jen Clarke is a seasoned performer and workshop leader with a passion for making comedy fun, accessible and inclusive. As Co-Director of _Absolute Onions_, founding cast member of _Improv on Demand_, cast member of _Smashed!_, _Drunk Classics_ and all-female troupe _Jens and Frens_, Jen brings years of experience in live performance, collaborative theatre-making, and nurturing new talent. Her sessions are joyful, welcoming, and packed with playful energy. She is passionate about mental health and wellbeing being embedded in all she does.
