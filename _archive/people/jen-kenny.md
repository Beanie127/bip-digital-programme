Jen has been improvising over 10 years. She teaches and performs improv in Northampton and Leicester with short form team _Canny Funny_, improvised musicals with _Rhymes Against Humanity_, and her own solo show _Mixtape_.

Musical improv is Jen’s passion and she loves teaching fellow improvisers to get over that fear of musical improv and find the pure joy that can come from creating a song from nothng.
