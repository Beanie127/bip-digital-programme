Graham is part of _Improv on Demand_, a short-form TV-themed troupe from Manchester. Graham started improvising in 2018 with _The Catalyst_ in Switzerland who use improv as a way to teach communication skills to scientists and engineers. Graham also writes and performs sketch comedy as part of _Standard Output_, as well as doing standup comedy.
