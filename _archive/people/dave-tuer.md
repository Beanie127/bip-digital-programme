Dave has been improvising since 2016. With a particular love for musical improv, he has trained with _MissImp_, _The Maydays_, and _Showstopper!_. His improv adventures have taken in five countries so far, including performances in Berlin and Edinburgh. He improvises songs of all styles with _Comic Harmonic_, emotionally-powerful drama with _Flower Freedom_, and a video game with Enter _Player 2_. He was longtime producer and compere of _Smash Night_ in Nottingham, and has taught with _MissImp_ since 2019.
