name: Wei Joo Ooi

Wei Joo led his first workshop in 2012 and amidst the chaos, people seemed to have fun! He got involved in the Reading improv scene in 2015 and later became a founding member of the *Free Radicals* troupe, using informative “TED-style” talks as the inspiration of their shows. Currently part of the longform group *Imagination Superhighway*, debuting at the Hoopla Improv Marathon with their Choose Your Own Adventure format, and horror based group *The Choir*.
