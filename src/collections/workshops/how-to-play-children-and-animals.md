In the acting world, they say you should never work with children or animals. But can you play them in an improv scene? 

Characters that aren’t human adults can give your scenes more heightened physicality and energy, richer perspectives and new territories for hilarity, but they can also distract and derail, create problematic situations or just leave you excluded from the action.  

In this workshop, we’ll look at the difference between *Rugrats* and *Bugsy Malone*, *Lassie* and *Narnia*, and what they mean for your improv; identify common pitfalls and practice techniques to avoid them; and play lots of strong characters that push you beyond your default.
