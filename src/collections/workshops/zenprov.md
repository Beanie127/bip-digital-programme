> ‘Be like water’ Bruce Lee.

In this workshop we’ll work on the interior and the exterior.

- First, we’ll aim for a Flow state of alert Effortlessness using Chi Kung & Pranayama (ancient breathing & movement techniques – MILD exertion).
- Secondly, from this flexible ‘Zen’-type state, we’ll use two performance tools to create fun but focused scenes, emphasising mutuality and collaboration.
- Finally, we'll have lots of easy fun with short-form games.

I hope you will gain experience/confidence in:

- your improv skills even when you try less
- using 2 specific performance tools to stay focused, keeping your scenes shiny, heated and engaging, learning to converge with fluidity & ease on a shared focal point.
