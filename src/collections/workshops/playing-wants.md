Sometimes we can establish the who, what and where of a scene but struggle to give it direction. This workshop explores how clear wants create momentum and purposeful action. Drawing on Stanislavski’s ideas about objectives and tactics, participants will practise communicating wants through behaviour, adapting tactics to obstacles, and recognising deeper wants. We'll explore how clear objectives can strengthen relationships, raise emotional stakes and drive scenes forward.
