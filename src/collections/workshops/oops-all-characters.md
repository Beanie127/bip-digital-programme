This workshop is an improviser's toolbox for characters! Learn some tips, tricks and techniques to have for making your characters bolder, more distinct, and feel more rounded on stage. Great for absolute beginners to those keen to sharpen their character work. Physical exertion at performers comfort level, and activities could be done seated if needed.
