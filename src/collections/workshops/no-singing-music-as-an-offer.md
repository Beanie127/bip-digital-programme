Music can be an incredibly helpful guide in improv, supporting improvisers to really step up and be braver, more expressive and more emotionally connected in their scenes. It can also give live performances a lovely edge.

We’ve gathered some fabulous exercises and games from far and wide that use music as an offer in a variety of interesting ways.
Let the music take the pressure off and let your improv fly.

This is open to improvisers of all levels, designed to let everyone shine.
