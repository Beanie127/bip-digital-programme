Theatrical clowning is the art of embracing vulnerability with absolute seriousness and creating a playful connection with the audience. It can be detailed, complicated and technical.

This workshop will teach participants the 3 fundamental skills of theatrical clowning through scenarios without the technical breakdown and at the end of the class, reveal what those techniques are.
