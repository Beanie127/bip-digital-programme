If you have never tried guessing games before, are a bit rusty, or love them and want to do more, this is the workshop for you!

This workshop will include some exercises to help you train skills that are useful for guessing games, tips and tricks to help your shows run smoothly, and will end with a chance to apply what you have learned with a simple game.
