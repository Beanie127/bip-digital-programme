Isn’t it magical when a scene seems real? There are two people on stage just reacting to each other and you can’t tell who’s making the offers; the whole thing seems to make itself. Let's practise that.

Your partner is making lots of non-verbal signals. The audience sees them and believes they mean something... and that how you behave in response means everything. Any words are secondary to the truth, so why struggle to make stuff up.

An exercise that takes ‘yes and’ and applies it to non verbal communication. Expect a slower session that involves looking closely at people, being looked at and a teacher who's apt to make up or adjust exercises to fit the room.
