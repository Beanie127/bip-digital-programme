Jon has been teaching and coaching improv for 16 years. He runs a weekly drop-in, workshops at all levels, and a 12-week evening class at a local arts centre. This year he delivered workshops at BIP, the Liverpool Improv Festival, ImproFest in Gothenburg and the Devon Improv Festival. Jon founded, directs and performs with _Box of Frogs_, Birmingham’s longest running shortform improv troupe. He co-founded, directs, and performs with _Breakfast of Champions_ longform group, who just got back from performing at the Sofia Improv Festival. He runs Birmingham Improv, and is the Director of the Birmingham Improv Festival.
