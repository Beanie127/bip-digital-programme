Stuart is part of _Sheep Tales_, who improvise comedy inspired by stories told by audience members. He has taught for the Oxford Improv Festival, Devon Improv Festival, Greater Manchester Improv Festival, Liverpool Comedy Improv, Dogface Improv and Hoopla Impro. He has been teaching improv for over ten years and has trained with Hoopla Impro, The Nursery, Showstoppers! The Improvised Musical, AndAlso Improv, David Razowsky and many more.
