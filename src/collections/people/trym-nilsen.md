Visiting from Oslo, Trym has been doing improv for about three years and absolutely loves it. They have previously performed at the Short Notice Improv Festival (SNIF) and the Oslo Improv Festival. Trym’s favourite thing about improv is finding the pure playfulness in a scene, a focus they try to actively bring to their own playing and facilitating. They truly believe improv is for everyone, simply because at its core, it's just play.
