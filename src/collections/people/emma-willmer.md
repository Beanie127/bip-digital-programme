Hello! I've been improvising for a long time. Dance, Playback theatre (psychodrama), and, my main love, comedy improv. I lead-teach the improv comedy _Gym Jam_ in Manchester, and 3 years ago, founded short-form comedy improv troupe _The Communimps_. I love helping improvisers do what they do, better. This is my 5th time, being honoured to teach you fabulous gang at the British Improv Project.
