Stags has performed stand-up for 2 decades and been running workshops/courses and running courses containing elements of basic shortform as a tool for stand-up for well over a decade, helping multiple people take shows and perform on the Fringe.

Finally making improv his main focus in 2018 and taking several improv shows to Edinburgh, including the popular _Spontadeity_ (mythology-based shortform), he also performs with longform troupes including _Breakfast of Champions_ & _Vox Pops_. He's also set up and runs several successful improv nights and courses.
