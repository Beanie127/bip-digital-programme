Stags has performed stand-up for 16+ years, making improv his main focus in 2018. He performs in longform troupes _Breakfast of Champions_, _Vox Pops_ & a twoprov with Jon Trevor: _Two Short Planks_ & just finished a 6th full Fringe run of _Spontadeity_ (mythology-based shortform).

Creator of several successful & inclusive improv nights: Derby’s _Chewy_, Nottingham’s _Petri Dish_ & 100+ online long-form sessions. Being AuDHD, he is particularly conscious of differing needs in groups.

He happily shares a wealth of learning from countless courses from experts on improv, clowning, acting & stand-up.
