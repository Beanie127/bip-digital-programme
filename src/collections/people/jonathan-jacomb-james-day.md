Jonathan and James have a collective 20 years improv experience.

Jonathan co-directs the Southend 48-hour improvathon and is instrumental to the improv scene in Southend by putting on jams, running specific workshops and workshopping a range of successful shows.

James frequently runs improvised singing classes and jams around London and has guested in a variety of shows.
