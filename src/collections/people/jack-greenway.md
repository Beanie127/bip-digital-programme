Jack is a Sheffield based improviser who has been a member of _Stürike Comedy_ since 2016. He got his start in shortform improv with the University of Sheffield's _The Shrimps_, and eventually went on to serve as the group's Artistic Director, leading workshops and hosting shows. Jack has since moved into long form improv with _Stürike Comedy_.
