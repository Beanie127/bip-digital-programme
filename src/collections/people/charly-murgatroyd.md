Charly started improv on a whim just after the pandemic. Splitting herself between Yorkshire and Merseyside, she doesn’t really have an improv home at present but has had serendipitous opportunities to perform at Improvfest Ireland and Liverpool Improvisation Festival in recent years. She can often be found making questionable physical choices for her scene partners to exploit and her to immediately regret such as the time she spent 20 minutes onstage crouching as a chair.
