_She/They_ | With nearly 14 years of improv under her belt, starting at the wildly overconfident age of 13, Jet Burnett brings a heap of UCB training and a major silly streak to the UK. Now based in Leamington with _Leamprov_, she stirs up mischief as a long-form regular and teacher to both short-form and long-form skills, helping new improvisers break out of their box. Her natural playground is narrative, character, and chaos.
