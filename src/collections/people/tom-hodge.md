Tom has been an improv musician for over 16 years, having worked with many top acts across the UK and beyond. A software developer in the audio industry by day, he can often be found improvising musicals with _Music Box_ or _Dragprov Revue_, evoking classic Hollywood with the improvised _'Talkies'_, playing for laughs with internationally renowned comedians, and providing dramatic tension for improvised murder mysteries in international waters with _Murder She Didn't Write_.
