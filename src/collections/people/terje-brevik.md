Terje Brevik is an improv nerd who teaches and performs improv for a living. He’s the founder of _Tøyen Impro_ and _SNIF_, the Short Notice Improv Festival (international workshops, shows, and a BIG DAY OUT). Over the past decade, he’s taught and performed in 11 countries. Terje lives in Oslo, Norway, and already regrets ending this sentence with cheese monster stapler butt.
