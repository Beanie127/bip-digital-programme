Improvises sketches and long form in _Shakespeare's First Drafts._

Was raised by Box Of Frogs and birthed by a cross of Jon Trevor and Keith Johnstone.

Hates seeing improvisers thinking on stage. Thinks words are the least interesting thing happening. Loves watching improvisers be surprised by what just emerges. In love with that moment when the scene takes you over. Is casting envious glances at masks, clowning and meisner.

Suspects improv has some aspects of a religion. Is cool with that.
