Welcome to our living room! Pull up a chair and make yourself at home as our cast settle in for a chat. Inspired by your suggestions, we will have a free-flowing, unfiltered on-stage conversation that at any moment could turn into a spontaneous improv scene! Just like a cosy evening in with the funniest people you know, who knows where the conversation – and the comedy – will take us?
