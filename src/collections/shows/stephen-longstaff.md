The world clearly hasn't enough Shakespeare, so this solo show improvises some more from audience suggestions. Your host (former university Shakespeare lecturer and current idiot) will transform whatever the Staffordshire muses bring into new iambic speeches and dialogues by some of the bard's most famous characters. It could get silly.
