Join The Langoustines as we explore a world very like our own but for one key difference that sends us on an entirely alien path. Perhaps it's the world where everyone's shadow is sentient? Maybe love is a solid object? What about the universe where scrambled eggs are running the government?!? Whatever it is, you'll find that the consequences of that one simple change are far more disastrous than you could ever have imagined!
