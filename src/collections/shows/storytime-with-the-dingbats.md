Veteran Dingbats improvisers Harry, Aimee, Liam and Fin are just champing at the bit to show off some narrative improv. These stories will be full to the brim with twists and turns of which you’ve never seen the like. There will be a beginning. There will be a middle. Heck, there’ll even be an end. By injecting their solid foundation in longform improv with bold narrative turning points, these utter Dingbats ensure a fun-filled act for the whole family (or just you, if you didn’t bring your family. That’s fine too.)
